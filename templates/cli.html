4orm cli

manage pages from your terminal

The 4orm CLI lets you publish pages, update them as you work, and upload media, right from the command line.

Download it, run 4orm login, and you are to go.

quick start

4orm login
4orm page publish about.html
4orm page publish notes.md
4orm media upload avatar.png

A file named about.html becomes the about page.

what you can do

  • publish HTML and Markdown pages
  • list and remove your pages
  • upload images, audio, and other media
  • work on your site without leaving your editor

Run 4orm login once, then use the commands whenever you want to update your site.
